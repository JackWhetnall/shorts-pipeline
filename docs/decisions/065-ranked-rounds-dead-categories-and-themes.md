# 065 — Rounds by rank when a level runs short, dead categories, and themes

**Status:** active. Amends [062](062-rounds-made-ahead.md).

## What happened

A round took ten questions within a tight band of its level's number on
the 1–10 scale. The rater calls few questions easy: Pub Quiz Buzz's Easy
sits at 1.5, and most categories had under twenty questions at 2 or
below. So:

- Easy rounds were rarely ready. Ancient History was written for
  sixteen times near Easy and still had none.
- A category without a round at every level can't plan its next set, so
  a dry Easy stopped the whole category's next set.
- It also stopped the category's easiest-to-hardest long quiz, which
  needs a round at every level. Long-form is the earner (owner,
  5 October).
- The writer kept being sent back to the slot, spending money on
  questions rated too hard for it.

Separately, a round could hold four questions on one theme. A round
spread its questions over "areas", and areas are free text: "World
capitals" and "European capitals" were different areas, so three capital
city questions could land in one Hard Geography round.

## What was decided

- **By rank when the band runs short.** When a level's own band can't
  make a round, the category's checked questions are ranked by level and
  cut into as many equal bands as the channel has levels. Easy takes the
  easiest band, Medium the next, and so on, widened to the neighbouring
  band if that one is used up. This was the owner's suggestion. The
  ranking is over every checked question in the category, used or not,
  so the bands don't drift as questions are used. The round is marked
  `relative`, and the topic plan shows "by rank: Easy".
- **Not when ranking means nothing:**
  - the channel has a single level;
  - the category has fewer questions than one full set;
  - the category's questions sit within half the channel's range (the
    middle 80% of them), since questions all rated alike have no
    easiest band.
- **Dead categories.** A category is dead when all three hold:
  - it can't make a set;
  - it has fewer questions left than a full set takes;
  - its last three write batches between them added fewer than five
    questions a round could use.

  It's flagged on the topic plan and on Today, and stocking stops
  writing for it. A category that's never been written for isn't dead,
  so a new category gets its chance.
- **Themes, not areas.** A round holds at most two questions sharing a
  theme. A theme is an area's meaningful words, stemmed:
  - place and period words are dropped ("World", "European"), so World
    and European capitals are one theme, "capital";
  - a word in more than 30% of the candidates' areas names the category
    rather than a theme ("music" in Music), so it's ignored.

  The cap loosens to three, and then to none, only when a round can't
  otherwise be filled.

On a copy of the live bank on 5 October, every Pub Quiz Buzz category
then had at least one full set. Ten had three. Maths was low, with 6
questions left.

## Not done, and why

- **Taking questions from a neighbouring category.** Questions are
  already filed under every category they fit (decision 060), so a Space
  question about an astronaut that also fits Science is in both pools.
  Taking ones that don't fit would put off-topic questions in a round.
- **Relabelling the bank's levels.** The rater's numbers stay as they
  are. Ranking is only a stand-in where they run out, and the absolute
  band is still tried first.
