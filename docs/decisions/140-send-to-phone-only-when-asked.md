# 140 — One video to the phone, only when asked

**Status:** active. Replaces 136.

## What the owner said (28 Oct)

> WHY HAVE ALL MY VIDEOS MOVED TO ONEDRIVE. THIS HAS COMPLETELY BROKEN MY
> POSTING-FROM-COMPUTER WORFLOW. put it back. ... i dont need every video
> ever to be in my onedrive. i never asked for that. i just wanted one
> video to be able to test from my phone. if i want to post something from
> my phone then i should be able to do that OPTIONALLY from the video
> posting page which should then make it available in onedrive.

Decision 136 answered a question about getting one test video onto the
phone by moving every waiting video into OneDrive. That changed the owner's
workflow without asking, and "Copy video location" stopped pointing where
they posted from.

## Decision

- **The folder stays where it was.** Videos waiting to be posted are in
  `Videos\Shorts to post` again, linked as before (decision 059). The 26
  that had moved were linked back and their OneDrive copies removed.
- **Send to phone** is a button on the To post page. It copies that one
  video into `OneDrive\Shorts to post` and then reads **On phone**.
- **When the video is posted everywhere or discarded,** its phone copy goes
  too.
- **Without OneDrive,** the button doesn't show.

The trailer made for the phone test stays in OneDrive, where the owner put
it to use.

## Lesson

A request about one video is answered for one video. A change to how the
owner already works is asked about first.
