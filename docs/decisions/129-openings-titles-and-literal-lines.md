# 129 — Openings that don't echo the title, one-phrase titles, lines that make sense

**Status:** active for the newsroom. Follows decisions 127 and 128.

## What the first day showed (22 Oct)

The first daytime runs took three films through writing. Three things went
wrong:

- **Every first proof was sent back,** four out of four, because its
  opening restated the title. The scout's pitched hook was usually the title
  reworded, and the writer followed it. Each rework cost about 3 points.
- **A garbled line got through.** The thunder film's third line, "Try it.
  Street number, street, town. Say it slowly, and the same flash is still
  going when you finish", passed both the edit and the final read.
- **Titles.** The thunder title was "Why Thunder Rumbles: It's the Bolt
  Drawn in Sound", a label and a subtitle. The sky title repeated what the
  film's first line says.

The owner: "yes tighten them, and fix the thunder line - any and all
improvements are welcome here".

## Decision

**Checks the desk makes** (`newsroom.echoes`, `newsroom.colon_title`):
- **Hooks and openings.** A pitched hook, a proof's opening or a draft's
  opening is refused when its first two sentences hold half or more of the
  title's key words. The Mars opening that lost half its viewers trips the
  check; the three new openings pass it.
- **Titles.** A pitch's or a package's title is refused if it has a colon,
  or if the film's first two sentences already say it.

**The roles' instructions:**
- **Scout and writer:** two good hooks from this newsroom as examples. The
  second sentence brings something new.
- **Writer:** every line must make sense on its own. Picture directions are
  one drawable moment, because the picture editor changed about two dozen
  pictures in each early film.
- **Script editor and managing editor:** read every line literally as a
  stranger; a garbled line goes back to `edit`.
- **Packaging editor:** one phrase that leads with the most surprising claim
  the film makes good on.

**The films already passed:**
- **The thunder line** now reads "[wry] Say your whole address out loud,
  slowly. When you get to the end, that one flash would still be rumbling."
  Its picture is unchanged.
- **Thunder title:** now "Thunder Is a Lightning Bolt Drawn in Sound", the
  commissioning editor's suggestion.
- **Sky title:** now "Why Is the Sky Blue When Violet Scatters More?", the
  scout's.

**Session cap:** raised to 60 points. One session stopped a point short of
a fourth film's draft.
