# 042 — Settings that apply, paintings as a medium, the quiz ladder, one way to choose a video

**Status:** active. Revises 038's paintings and 041's quiz writing.

## What happened

After rendering the first quiz, the owner raised four problems.

1. **The footage/graphics slider made no sense on a quiz**, and neither
   did animation style. The only clue was the format setting under
   Channel. Options a channel can't use are misleading.
2. **Paintings read as a Bible feature.** The setting said "a painting of
   the passage while it's read". It was a separate stage that pictured one
   segment only: the passage, or a topic channel's opening. The owner had
   assumed paintings were a kind of picture any video could use sometimes.
3. **Quiz difficulty didn't build.** A Medium made first gave no sign that
   Hard would be harder. Each round was written alone, knowing only which
   questions not to repeat. The owner wanted scripts written in ladder
   order and videos made in random order.
4. **The create page was confusing:**
   - it showed eight topics beside a different random pick;
   - clicking a topic re-rolled the pick instead of following it;
   - "Open the full plan" left the page;
   - "Make next" on the plan did nothing visible. It moved the subtopic up
     the queue, which a channel that picks at random ignores.

## What was decided

**A setting a channel can't use isn't shown.** A quiz channel's picture
section becomes "Board look":

- its colours and fonts, previewed on a sample quiz board;
- no footage slider, motion-graphics sheet, illustration style or
  paintings;
- no hook text or pop-in words in Look.

Hidden values are kept, so switching the format back loses nothing.

**Paintings are one of the director's media** ("artwork"), allowed per
channel:

- used for any segment a well-known public-domain painting or engraving
  shows better than footage: history, myth, scripture, literature, art;
- briefed as a museum search;
- a real picture, like footage, so the graphics slider doesn't gate it;
  it needs no director call on a footage-only channel unless allowed;
- credits travel on the clip, so a resumed render keeps them;
- the old passage stage and the script's painting search are gone. The
  old "passage" setting reads as allowed, and drafts can propose it.

**A quiz category's rounds are written up the ladder, and made in any
order.**

- Making a round first writes and stores any easier round of the same
  category (in the same round of the plan) that has neither a script nor
  a video. Easiest goes first.
- Each round's prompt shows the other rungs' questions and answers,
  labelled as easier or harder, and asks for a clear step between them.
- A round already made counts as a rung.
- Stored rounds are used when their video comes up, and count as asked so
  nothing repeats.
- The plan page's "Write scripts" writes a category's ladder in order.
  Regenerating a rung rewrites it against the others.
- The extra cost is small and not wasted: rounds are written earlier than
  their videos, not additionally.

**Create video shows one chosen video and the whole plan.**

- The chosen video always sits with the one button that makes it.
- Everything else only changes which video that is:
  - next up;
  - another at random;
  - at random from a topic;
  - a specific waiting video, from every topic, with a filter.
- For a quiz it says which easier rounds will be written first.
- On the plan page, "Make now" opens it with that video chosen.
- "Up next" marks a subtopic that the ordering then honours before its
  own rules, until it's made.

## Tried and rejected

- **Disabling the controls rather than hiding them.** A disabled input
  submits nothing, which the settings form reads as "cleared".
- **Writing a category's whole ladder when the channel is created.**
  With 15 categories × 5 levels, that's 75 rounds (about $4) spent before
  the channel has made a single video.
