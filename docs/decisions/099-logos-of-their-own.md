# 099 — Logos that look like their own channel

**Status:** active.

## What happened

The owner made a logo for Why, Though? and saw no options appear. They
also noticed that every channel's logo looked alike.

## Why no options appeared

The five ideas had been made and saved. The page only showed them in a
section revealed by script, below the button, without scrolling to it.
Reloading the page never showed ideas already made, so it looked as if
nothing had happened.

Now:
- The page lists any ideas already made.
- After making new ones, it scrolls to them.
- The button shows the price: five at $0.042 each, $0.21.

## Why the logos were all alike

The prompt coached every channel the same way:
- "shading, gradients, and layered detail";
- "a full, vivid color palette";
- a centred mark on a plain background.

All five ideas came from that one prompt, in one request. So the
Pub Quiz Buzz and Why, Though? logos came out the same glossy orange
sticker on a navy disc, with a dark vignette. Minute Pastor's and Wren's
had that look too, in different colours. The five options in a batch
were near-copies of each other.

## Decision

- **The prompt keeps only rules that apply to any logo:**
  - one clear idea that reads as a small round profile picture;
  - colours chosen for the subject;
  - no text, and no platform symbols;
  - a background that is part of the design.
- **Each idea takes a different direction from `DIRECTIONS`**, a pool of
  twelve looks, each with its own medium and colour approach. Examples:
  two-colour flat, negative space, paper collage, monoline, woodcut,
  risograph, geometric emblem, brush and ink, matte clay, mid-century,
  stained glass, pixel art.
- **One image per direction**, with the five requests sent at once. The
  cost is unchanged ($0.21).
- **A live batch for Why, Though? came back varied:** an engraving, a
  negative-space mark, matte clay, a geometric emblem and a risograph
  print. The subject itself (a question mark and a globe) still comes
  from the brief. A more specific brief gives more specific marks.
