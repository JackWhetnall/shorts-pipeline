# 001 — ElevenLabs for text-to-speech

**Status:** active

## Decision

Narration is generated by ElevenLabs' `with-timestamps` endpoint.

## Why

The predecessor was `edge-tts`, which was free but sounded noticeably
robotic and had occasional streaming glitches that produced duplicated
audio. Those weren't fixable from our side: it's a reverse-engineered
wrapper around a free consumer feature, not a supported API, so there was
no contract to hold it to and no way to report a fault.

The timestamps endpoint returns character-level alignment against the
exact text sent. Word timings come from grouping runs of non-whitespace
characters, which keeps punctuation attached to its word automatically —
no fuzzy re-matching to recover it, and no separate forced-alignment
step.

## Cost

It is the main ongoing expense, billed per character. `core/costs.py`
records it per video.
