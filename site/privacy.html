Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Shorts Pipeline is a personal tool, run by one person on their own computer, for making short videos and uploading them to that person's own YouTube channels. It has no other users. This policy says what it does with the Google and YouTube data it is given access to.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Google asks for permission, and the app receives:

  • the Google account's email address, to show which account is connected;
  • permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel;
  • permission to read the channel's details (its name and identifier) and read-only YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos (views, watch time and similar figures).

It does not access anyone else's account, the owner's contacts, files, or any other Google service.

What it does with that data

  • The upload permission is used only to upload videos the owner has made and approved, with the title and description the owner has set.
  • The read permissions are used only to show the owner how their own videos are doing, inside the app.
  • The data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI model.
  • Nobody reads the data other than the owner.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The sign-in tokens Google issues are saved in a file on that computer and are used only to talk to Google's YouTube services. They are not sent to any server run by the app, because the app has none. The video figures it reads are kept in the same place.

Keeping and deleting it

The tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the channel in the app, which deletes them. The owner can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is removed, the app can no longer upload or read anything.

Google's requirements

Shorts Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
