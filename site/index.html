Shorts Pipeline

A personal tool for making short vertical videos and uploading them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

Shorts Pipeline turns a topic (a set of quiz questions, say) into a finished short video: it writes the script, records the narration, adds pictures and captions, and renders the video. The owner reviews each video and approves it, and the app then uploads it to a YouTube channel the owner has connected.

How it uses YouTube and Google

The owner signs in with Google once per channel, and the app is then allowed to:

  • Upload videos to that channel (the videos the owner has approved);
  • Read the channel's name and the view and engagement figures of its own videos, so the owner can see how they are doing.

The app runs on the owner's own computer. It has no public website, no other users and no accounts. Nothing from a Google account is sent anywhere except back to Google's own services.
