Step {{ step_index + 1 }}: Set up Amazon Associates

Amazon's affiliate program. A quick rundown since it's not obvious:

  1. Apply at Amazon Associates with your channel as the traffic source (you'll need an existing channel/site, not a blank application).
  2. Once approved, you get a single reusable tracking ID (looks like yourtag-20) — this is what actually earns you commission, not a one-time link.
  3. To turn any Amazon product page into an affiliate link, append ?tag=yourtag-20 to its URL yourself, or use Amazon's own "SiteStripe" toolbar (appears at the top of product pages once you're logged in as an Associate) to generate the link for you.
  4. Paste 2-3 evergreen product links below (the same items every video can point to, rather than something per-video).

Worth knowing — the tracking cookie only lasts 24 hours (90 days if the item's added to a cart first); Amazon closes accounts that don't get 3 qualifying purchases within 180 days of approval; and disclosure is required wherever these links appear (already handled automatically — see every generated video's description).
